# Phase 12 - Natural Language

## Objective

Allow users to enter expenses using natural language, such as "Spent ₹250 on lunch" or "Paid ₹900 electricity bill yesterday."

## What We Build

Natural language transaction parser, entity extraction for amount, date, and category, and quick natural text input.

## Expected Result

Natural-language input can be converted into structured expense information.
